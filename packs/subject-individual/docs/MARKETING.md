# Individual workspaces
> A personal workspace for every user: one person, one profile, their own private data.

## Who it's for
Consumer and prosumer apps where each customer is a person: job seekers, creators, hobbyists, freelancers.

## What you get
- Every signup can create a personal workspace that all other packs attach to.
- Optional personal details (headline, location, links) kept private to the owner.
- A per-plan cap on how many personal workspaces one account may have.

## Works well with
item-tracker, social-posts, posthog-analytics.

## Under the hood
Row-level security on every table, column-level grants, no anonymous access, MFA-aware policies. Removing the pack leaves your data model untouched.
