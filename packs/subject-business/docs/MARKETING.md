# Business workspaces
> A workspace for a company, shop or practice, with a public-facing business profile.

## Who it's for
Apps that serve small businesses: local services, agencies, restaurants, clinics, real-estate offices.

## What you get
- Business workspaces with category, phone, website and structured address.
- Plan-based limit on how many businesses one account can run.
- Pairs with Team so a business can have staff, not just an owner.

## Works well with
team, real-estate-listings, social-posts, loops-email.

## Under the hood
Same secure-by-default rules as every pack. Can coexist with Individual workspaces in the same app.
