# Connect your Facebook page to your store: optional Graph route (DRAFT 2026-10-04)

**Not the default.** Shops normally only give us their page address and we read public posts (ScrapeCreators, `docs/OFFER-SYNC.md`). Use this only if a shop wants us to read through Facebook's official API.

For the shop owner. Time: 2 minutes. You do not need a developer account, an app, or any key. You only let your RapScalYon operator see your page's posts.

1. On Facebook, open your business Page and switch to it (tap your Page's name, then **Switch now**). **[verify: wording differs between phone and computer]**
2. Open **Settings** (Settings & privacy, then Settings), then **Page access** (or **Page setup**, then **People with Facebook access** / **People with task access**). **[verify: exact menu names]**
3. Click **Add new**, search for our operator's Facebook name (we give it to you), and choose **Task access**. Turn on only what lets us read your posts (for example **Content** or **Insights**). Leave everything else off, in particular **Messages**, **Ads** and **Page roles**. **[verify: the smallest task set that lets the API read posts]**
4. Confirm with your Facebook password if asked. We accept the invitation on our side.

That is all. Nothing is published to your Page and nothing goes live in your store without your approval. To stop it any time, remove our name in the same screen.

Please do not send us your Facebook password, and never send a code or a link that contains "access_token".
